Unity Shortcuts & Tips

Quick reference for Unity keyboard shortcuts by workspace. Search or filter by mode.

Scene Navigation Mastery
Use Middle Mouse Button drag to orbit freely around objects. Scroll wheel zooms in/out smoothly. Press F to instantly frame your selection, then Shift+F to lock the camera follow mode for continuous tracking.
Rapid GameObject Workflow
W/E/R/T toggle between Move, Rotate, Scale, and Rect Transform tools instantly. Ctrl+D duplicates selected objects. Ctrl+Shift+N creates empty GameObjects. Double-click objects in the hierarchy to ping and select them.
Animation & Inspector Tips
Shift+K adds keyframes in the Animation window. Use Comma/Period to navigate frames, Alt+Comma/Period to jump between keyframes. Ctrl+Shift+C quickly adds components from the Inspector without opening dropdowns.
