Texture Mapping & UV Coordinates

Texture mapping is the process of applying a 2D image (texture) onto a 3D surface. This interactive guide explores UV coordinates, different texture types, wrapping modes, and projection methods. All textures are generated procedurally for instant, reliable rendering.

How it works: UV coordinates map 2D texture space onto 3D geometry. The U axis typically corresponds to horizontal (X), and V to vertical (Y). Scale adjusts how many times the texture repeats, while offset shifts the pattern. Different projection modes unwrap 3D shapes differently for optimal texture placement.
