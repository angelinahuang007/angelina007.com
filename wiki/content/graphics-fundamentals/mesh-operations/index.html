Operations

Subdivide: Adds new vertices at edge midpoints and face centers, then moves old vertices.

Simplify: Removes edges by collapsing vertices together, reducing polygon count.

Smooth: Moves vertices toward the average of their neighbors (Laplacian smoothing).
