3D scene is rendered to the G-Buffer. Each pixel stores material properties and geometry information.

Deferred Rendering Advantages

Decoupling: Geometry pass doesn't know about lights. Allows many lights efficiently.

Scalability: Light count doesn't affect G-Buffer complexity.

Trade-off: Higher bandwidth (multiple render targets), challenges with MSAA.
