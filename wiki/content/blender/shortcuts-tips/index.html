Blender Shortcuts & Tips

Quick reference for Blender keyboard shortcuts by mode. Search or filter by mode.

Faster Workflow
Use Tab to toggle Object/Edit mode instantly. Hold Shift while clicking to select multiple objects. Press Numpad . to focus on selection.
Precision Transforms
After pressing G/R/S, type a number for exact values (e.g., S 2 Enter = scale 2x). Add axis constraint by pressing X/Y/Z after the transform key.
Hidden Gems
Ctrl+Shift+M for Merge by Distance (clean duplicates). Alt+Z toggles X-ray mode. Shift+S opens the Snap menu for precise cursor/selection placement.
